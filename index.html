关于转型的思考 On Career Transition

为什么决定不接着干码农了呢？ In short：时代变化的推动 + 个人性格的回归。

一方面，近年来飞速发展的 AI coding 能力使得纯粹的代码技术变得越来越廉价。Claude Opus 4.5 的重磅发布以及 MCP/Skills 等技术的出现，证实了 Coding Agent 已经可以从需求理解、架构分析、代码编写、测试用例生成、构建调试、自主修复、上线监控等流程上实现闭环。我坚信旧事物的崩塌必然伴随着新事物的诞生，转型阵痛的同时也一定伴随着新的机遇。

未来最重要的能力是 Vision 和 Taste。在模糊的环境下，总能做出那个"对"的选择——在不确定性中勇往直前！

另一方面，我打小就嘴贫话多坐不住，梦想有一天可以滔滔不绝地给各种人讲。PDSA 这种岗位又能说话，又能看技术，又能看项目，又能天南海北到处跑，真是不错诶，大喜，遂冲！

Why did I stop being a full-time software engineer? In short: the push of a changing era + a return to my natural personality.

On one hand, the rapid development of AI coding capabilities has made pure coding skills increasingly commoditized. The release of Claude Opus 4.5 and technologies like MCP/Skills have proven that Coding Agents can already achieve end-to-end automation across requirement analysis, architecture design, code generation, testing, debugging, and deployment. I firmly believe that the collapse of old paradigms inevitably brings the birth of new ones — with pain comes new opportunity.

The most important abilities for the future are Vision and Taste. Being able to consistently make the "right" choice in ambiguous environments — charging forward through uncertainty!

On the other hand, I've always been talkative and restless since childhood, dreaming of a day when I could speak passionately to all kinds of people. The PDSA role lets me talk, explore technology, manage projects, and travel — the perfect fit!
